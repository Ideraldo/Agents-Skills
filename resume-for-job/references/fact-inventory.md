# Fact inventory

The single source of truth for what any resume version may say. Audit it line by line with Claude once, then keep it updated. Replace the examples below with your own.

For each item, write what you did **and** what you must never claim about it. The "never" lines are what stop tailoring from drifting into exaggeration.

## Company A, Role, remote, Jan/2024 to present

- Built X with Y for Z. **Owned end to end.**
- Took part in the architecture discussion for W. **Did not design it**: proposed a different approach that was not adopted. Never use "designed" or "architected" here. (Great interview story about technical disagreement; bad resume bullet.)
- Implemented the retrieval pipeline of a RAG assistant, sharing the work with a senior dev. **Implemented, did not decide.** Never "owned" or "designed".
- Cut a report from ~2 minutes to near-instant with a local cache. Never "distributed cache".
- Rework dropped after adopting process P. **Perception, not measured**: never a number.
